what you ask.

Nine questions that come up often. Click to open an answer. If yours isn't here, write to us: we read everything.

How do I book a session?

+

Straight online. The "Book an appointment" button on the home page opens both calendars. "Book a session" to come in and record, mix or produce. "Discovery Call" if you'd rather talk it through first. Take the slot that suits you and it's confirmed right away.

How much does a session cost?

+

Recording is CHF 70.- per hour, billed by the hour started. Mixing starts at CHF 350.- per track, two revisions included. Depending on the project, stems, edit or arrangement may be added. We don't master in-house: we work with partner engineers. Full productions and mastering are both quoted. The detail is in the services grid.

What is your cancellation policy?

+

A session cancelled less than 48 hours before the start is charged in full. Beyond 48 hours, no charge. A serious emergency can always be discussed. But the studio is held for you and the slot stays reserved.

What should I bring on the day?

+

Your instruments. Your sonic references: Spotify playlist, tracks that inspire you. Your lyrics or charts if you sing or play written music. And whatever you need to settle in. We have everything else: mics, preamps, recording chain. Cables, stands, headphones are on us.

How long does a mix take?

+

It depends on the density of the track and on the slots we have open, so we don't give a deadline up front. We talk it through on the discovery call or in session. We then commit to a date we can hold. The process itself doesn't change: we deliver a v1 mix, listen together (or remotely via a link), you flag what feels off, we make a v2. Two revisions are included, so v3 is the final version. Beyond that, each extra revision is charged CHF 50.-.

Do we keep our stems and multitracks?

+

Yes, on request. On delivery you leave with the final mix. The stems (instrument groups) and the full session multitracks are provided if you ask for them. The master comes afterwards, if you go through our partners. It's your work and you own it.

Can we talk before booking?

+

Yes. The discovery call runs 30 minutes, free and with no commitment. We go over your project, what you need and the kind of session that fits. It books online like a session, from the home page. It is a call: there is no studio visit at that stage.

What styles do you work with?

+

We record and produce every style: pop, rap, soul, chanson, indie, electronic.

How does payment work?

+

No payment goes through the site: booking a slot charges nothing. For a recording session, settlement is by invoice. For a larger project a deposit may be requested, but only after the discovery call and against a quote. Payment by bank transfer (Swiss IBAN) or Twint. For longer projects (albums), we can split into milestones.
