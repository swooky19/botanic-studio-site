Recording studio · Lausanne

The Studio.

A space built for sound work to be its own experience. Two booths, a live room, analog and digital gear chosen for its musicality — not for spec sheets. Every project gets the same attention, whether it's a first single or a fifth album.

Main booth

A warm sound,
by nature.

Ribbon and condenser microphones for the warmth of an analog sound. The choice of mic shapes the sound as much as the talent in front of it — we take the time to find the right combination.

The atmosphere

A no-judgment
space.

A session is a place where risk-taking is welcome. We work fast when we have to, take our time when the sound asks for it. The atmosphere isn't a detail — it's half the work.

Control room

Two desks.
One flow.

The control room is set up to work cleanly, without losing time between ideas. The signal chain is short, calibrated, and reproducible from session to session.

The team
